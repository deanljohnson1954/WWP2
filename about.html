A Distinctive Professional Foundation

Withbert W. Payne, CPA received his early education at Oak Grove Boys' School in the Himalayas, earning his General Certificate of Education in Mathematics from the University of Cambridge. He subsequently trained as a Chartered Accountant at H. Foulkes Lynch School of Accountancy in London, earning his Fellowship of the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales — the FCA designation — one of the most demanding and internationally respected professional accounting qualifications in the world.

That formation — Cambridge, the ICAEW, and early training in the rigorous analytical standards of British professional accounting — established the intellectual framework that defines every client engagement today.

Insurance Review Services

What We Do — and How We Do It

Insurance Review Services provides independent, CPA-level reviews of life, disability, long-term care, and business insurance for professionals, executives, business owners, and families throughout the San Francisco Bay Area. Our objective is straightforward: maximize benefit, minimize cost, and ensure that no premium dollar is wasted.

Our Objective

Maximize Benefit. Minimize Cost.

We identify insurance structures that minimize our clients' costs and maximize their benefits — accomplished through specialized product knowledge and long-standing relationships with leading carriers. In many cases, we identify coverage structures that provide a full return of all premiums paid, less any benefits received. The premiums you pay are never simply lost.

Our track record reflects this focus: a 30–40% average improvement in policy efficiency across client engagements. In one recent case, a policy review resulted in a $3.8 million increase in guaranteed death benefit — with no material increase in premiums.

Our Approach

Analyze. Design. Coordinate.

Every engagement begins with a thorough analysis of the client's existing coverage, financial profile, and protection objectives. We design a responsible program and recommend a cost-effective strategy tailored to the individual situation.

Where appropriate, we coordinate directly with the client's CPA, attorney, and wealth advisor — ensuring that insurance analysis integrates fully with the broader financial and estate plan. Mr. Payne personally participates in every review.

Why Independent Review Matters

A Second Opinion Belongs in Every Insurance Decision

To avoid a conflict of interest, your coverage should be evaluated by someone other than the agent who sold it. The selling agent has an inherent interest in defending the existing policy. An independent review has no such constraint.

Withbert W. Payne, CPA personally conducts every review — applying the same independent analytical framework developed at Price Waterhouse and refined across more than 40 years of finance, audit, and insurance experience.

No Fee
All policy reviews are provided at no charge to the client. Insurance Review Services is compensated directly by the insurance carriers.

Personal
