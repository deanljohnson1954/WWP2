LTC Planning

Customizable Solutions

Long-term care planning is not one-size-fits-all. These eight flexible options let you build a strategy precisely matched to your income, assets, family situation, and goals.

Your long-term care plan can take advantage of a wide range of customizable funding and coverage options — from whole life and annuity-based solutions to qualified retirement dollars, joint coverage, and flexible payment structures. Each option below can be tailored to your specific needs.
01
Policy Structure
Whole Life & Annuity-Based Options
Available for applicants ages 35 to 80, whole life insurance and annuity-based LTC solutions protect your income, your loved ones, and your legacy — while providing guaranteed long-term care coverage that cannot be cancelled as long as premiums are paid.
02
Benefit Flexibility
Claims Payment Flexibility — Cash Indemnity
When a client wants care delivered at home by an informal caregiver — and that choice is aligned with their plan of care — a Cash Indemnity Benefit can be paid directly to support that caregiver. This gives families the freedom to choose who provides care, not just where.
The LTC pool can be accessed at up to 75% of the monthly benefit limit — paid directly to offset the real costs of informal home care.
03
Coverage Duration
Lifetime Protection
Coverage that never runs out — lifetime protection for qualifying long-term care needs eliminates financial worry about what may come or how long care may last. Whether a care event lasts months, years, or decades, your benefits remain intact.
1 in 5 people will need care for 5 or more years. Lifetime coverage ensures you are never left without protection, regardless of how long care is needed.
04
Cost Certainty
Guaranteed Benefits & Guaranteed Costs
Premiums that will not increase. Benefits that will not decrease — ever. This is the financial certainty that standalone LTC policies have often failed to deliver. Your coverage amount and your cost are both locked in at the time of issue.
05
Payment Structure
Flexible Funding — Lump Sum or Over Time
Pay in a single lump sum for immediate full coverage — ideal for repositioning an existing asset such as a CD, savings account, or annuity. Or spread payments over time with a structured premium schedule that fits your cash flow. You choose the structure that works for your financial plan.
06
Tax Advantage
Qualified Dollars Count — 401(k), IRA & Annuity Funding
Clients can fund their LTC protection using pre-tax dollars from traditional 401(k)s, IRAs, or other qualified retirement accounts — making every dollar work harder. Non-qualified annuities can also be used, providing a tax-efficient way to reposition existing assets into guaranteed LTC protection.
Using qualified retirement funds to pay premiums may reduce your taxable income in the year of transfer — consult your CPA for specifics.
07
Couple Strategy
Joint Coverage — One Policy, Shared Benefits
A single policy can cover both spouses or partners under a shared benefit structure — often at a lower combined cost than two separate policies. If one partner exhausts their individual benefit, the shared pool may provide additional coverage, protecting both individuals under one streamlined plan.
08
Support Service
Caregiver Consultant — Included with Cash Indemnity
While receiving a Cash Indemnity Benefit, informal caregivers have access to a dedicated caregiver consultant who provides insights, education, and emotional support through optional virtual or in-person monthly touchpoints. This resource helps ease the physical and emotional burdens of caregiving — protecting both the person receiving care and the family member providing it.
The caregiver consultant is not an add-on — it is included as part of the Cash Indemnity benefit, at no additional cost.
