For assisted living, skilled nursing, and all other facility-based care, a 90-day waiting period applies — measured within 270 calendar days — before benefits are paid.

Continuation of Benefits (COB) Rider

What Happens If My AOB Balance Runs Out?

The optional Continuation of Benefits Rider provides a critical safety net — if your Acceleration of Benefits (AOB) pool is fully exhausted from qualifying LTC expenses, the COB Rider steps in and extends your LTC benefits beyond the original policy pool.

Think of the COB Rider as a second reservoir of LTC funding that activates automatically once the primary pool is depleted — so your care never stops just because the original benefit runs out.

COB Duration Options

LifetimeBenefits continue for the rest of your life — no matter how long care is needed.

COB Rider — Funding Frequency

How Do I Pay for the COB Rider?

The Continuation of Benefits Rider can be funded in two ways — a single one-time payment, or a structured recurring premium schedule that spreads the cost over time.

Option A
Single Premium
Pay once at the time of policy issue — fully fund the COB Rider with a single lump sum and never pay again. Ideal for asset repositioning or single-premium strategies.
Option B
Recurring Premium
Spread the cost of the COB Rider over time with a structured payment schedule. Choose the period that fits your cash flow:
5-Pay 10-Pay 20-Pay Pay-to-95

International Facility Coverage

Care Outside the United States

Coverage is provided for facility care received in locales outside the U.S., its territories, and Canada. There is no international coverage for non-facility care.

Benefit Amount
The monthly benefit is 50% of the initial AOB monthly benefit limit. Example: if the base contract provides $2,000/month, the international benefit will be $1,000/month. May vary by state.
Benefit Triggers
Triggers do not differ for international coverage — the same 2-of-6 ADL standard and cognitive impairment criteria apply worldwide.
Canada: Not treated as international coverage — all forms of contractual care are eligible. Benefits are reimbursement-based; exchange rate conversion from Canadian to US dollars will apply.
Benefits are only payable under the AOB (base contract). International facility benefits are not payable under the COB Rider.
