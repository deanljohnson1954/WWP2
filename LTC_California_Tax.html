California and federal tax laws create powerful deduction opportunities for LTC insurance premiums — especially for C-Corporation owners, who may deduct up to 100% of the LTC care portion of the premium.

Individual & Self-Employed Deductibility

Federal Tax Deduction — Individuals

Premiums paid for a tax-qualified LTC insurance policy may be deductible as a medical expense on your federal return, subject to age-based limits set by the IRS. For 2026, the eligible premium limits per person are:

Age 51–60
$1,690/yr
Age 61–70
$4,510/yr
Age 71+
$6,020/yr
Age 41–50
$850/yr
Deductions are subject to the 7.5% AGI floor for medical expenses. Self-employed individuals may deduct 100% of eligible LTC premiums above the line — consult your CPA for specifics.

C-Corporation — Maximum Tax Advantage

C-Corp Owner — Two Layers of Tax Savings

For a C-Corporation owner, the LTC care portion of the premium qualifies for a 100% business deduction — with no age-based limits. This creates two distinct layers of real tax savings:

Example — California C-Corp · LTC Care Premium: $101,397

⚠
Important: If the C-Corporation has no taxable income, there will be no tax benefit. Tax savings are only realized to the extent the corporation has taxable income in the year of the deduction. Consult your CPA or tax advisor.

California State Partnership Program

Protect Assets — Qualify for Medi-Cal Sooner

California participates in the Long-Term Care Partnership Program, which allows policyholders with a qualifying LTC policy to protect assets equal to the benefits paid out by the policy — dollar for dollar — when applying for Medi-Cal (California's Medicaid program).

Example: If your LTC policy pays out $200,000 in benefits, you can protect $200,000 in assets and still qualify for Medi-Cal — without spending those assets down. This effectively gives every dollar of LTC coverage double duty: it pays for care AND shields your estate.
  • ✓Policy must be California Partnership-certified at time of purchase
  • ✓Dollar-for-dollar asset protection matching benefits paid
  • ✓Reciprocal — protection recognized in other participating states
  • ✓Inflation protection required for ages under 76 at purchase

Key Tax Facts — 2026

  • C-Corp: 100% deduction on LTC care portion — no age limits
  • Individual: Age-based eligible premium limits (up to $6,020/yr age 71+)
  • Self-employed: 100% above-the-line deduction on eligible amounts
  • California follows federal qualified LTC policy definitions
  • Partnership Program: dollar-for-dollar Medi-Cal asset protection
  • No tax benefit if C-Corp has no taxable income
