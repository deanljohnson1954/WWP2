What to Expect

A Consultative, Not Transactional, Conversation

The initial consultation is focused on understanding your situation — not on presenting products. It typically covers:

  • Your retirement income structure and asset base
  • Family history and care risk factors
  • Current insurance or LTC coverage, if any
  • Planning objectives and concerns
  • Whether a formal analysis and illustration is appropriate
